What we do

Three ways we work

Training for individuals

Hands-on Generative AI training for developers and practitioners — scheduled courses and private expert sessions that go past the demos into how these systems actually behave.

Training for organizations

Structured programmes for teams adopting AI in their day-to-day work, shaped around what your people build and the tools they already use.

Educational software

We design and publish our own learning products for web and mobile, built around how people genuinely practise and retain what they learn rather than around volume of content.

The company

Small by design, deliberate by habit.

Gyankosh takes its name from the Sanskrit ज्ञानकोश — a treasury of knowledge. The work follows from that: build things worth learning from, and teach them properly.

We keep the operation small and the standards high. Everything we publish is our own — designed, built, and shipped end to end rather than assembled to a brief.
